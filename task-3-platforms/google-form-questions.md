# Task 3 – Digital Literacy Awareness Quiz (Google Form)

## Google Form: "Digital Literacy Awareness Quiz"

This form was created to quiz batchmates on Digital Literacy basics as part of my Digital Ambassador role.

---

### Question 1 (Multiple Choice)
**What does "Digital Literacy" mean?**

- A) Knowing how to type fast on a keyboard
- B) The ability to use digital tools effectively, safely, and responsibly ✅
- C) Having access to a smartphone or laptop
- D) Knowing how to code in Python

---

### Question 2 (Multiple Choice)
**Which of the following is a sign of a phishing email?**

- A) It comes from your college's official domain
- B) It has a clear sender name and no attachments
- C) It asks you to click a link and enter your password urgently ✅
- D) It contains your correct name and registration number

---

### Question 3 (Multiple Choice)
**Which platform is best suited for building a professional career profile and connecting with recruiters?**

- A) Instagram
- B) Snapchat
- C) LinkedIn ✅
- D) Reddit

---

### Question 4 (Multiple Choice)
**What is two-factor authentication (2FA)?**

- A) Using two different passwords for the same account
- B) Logging in from two different devices simultaneously
- C) An extra security step that verifies your identity using a second method (e.g., OTP) ✅
- D) A method to recover a forgotten password

---

### Question 5 (Short Answer)
**In your own words, describe one good habit a college student should follow to stay safe online.**

*(Open-ended – responses recorded in linked Google Sheet)*

## Platform Reflection

For this task I used two platforms: **HackerRank** for coding practice and **Google Forms** for building the quiz.

On HackerRank, I created an account and completed the "Solve Me First" beginner challenge under the Problem Solving section. This challenge introduced me to reading input and producing output in a structured way. Even though I am not from a CS background, the platform made it very approachable for beginners, and I could see myself using it regularly to strengthen my programming fundamentals.

For Google Forms, I designed a 5-question quiz that can realistically be shared with my batchmates to test their awareness of digital safety, phishing, and professional platforms. The linked Google Sheet automatically captures all responses, making it easy to analyse the results. These tools — Google Forms and Sheets together — are incredibly useful for collecting feedback, running surveys, and conducting peer assessments in an academic setting. I plan to use Google Workspace tools throughout my degree for group projects and data collection.

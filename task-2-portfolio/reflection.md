# Task 2 – Student Digital Portfolio

## Platforms Set Up

I created and updated profiles on the following three platforms:

1. **GitHub** (github.com)
2. **LinkedIn** (linkedin.com)
3. **Kaggle** (kaggle.com)

---

## Platform Descriptions and Purpose

**GitHub** is a cloud-based platform for hosting and collaborating on code repositories. It is widely used by developers, students, and researchers to share projects and contribute to open-source software. For me, GitHub will serve as my main technical portfolio — every project I build during my B.Tech will be stored and showcased here.

**LinkedIn** is a professional networking platform used globally by students, working professionals, and recruiters. It allows you to list your education, skills, projects, and certifications. I added my current degree (B.Tech – ECE), institution (VIT Bhopal University), and expected graduation year. LinkedIn will help me connect with internship recruiters and alumni as I progress through my degree.

**Kaggle** is a platform focused on data science, machine learning, and AI. It hosts datasets, competitions, and courses. Even as an ECE student, data skills are increasingly valuable, and Kaggle will help me practice and build a portfolio in data analytics.

---

## Reflection

Over the next four years, I plan to use GitHub actively to host all my mini-projects, lab work, and final year project. I will update LinkedIn every semester with new skills and certifications, and use it to apply for internships in my second and third year. Kaggle will help me explore the intersection of ECE and data science. Setting up these profiles early felt like laying the foundation for a professional identity — something I previously had not thought about as a first-year student.

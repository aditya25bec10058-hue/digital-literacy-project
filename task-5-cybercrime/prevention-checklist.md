# Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist
### For College Students in India

---

## General Online Safety

1. **Always verify the sender's email address** before clicking any link or downloading any attachment. Official emails from government bodies end in .gov.in — not .com or .net variations.

2. **Never share OTPs with anyone** — not even someone claiming to be from your bank, college, or government department. No legitimate institution will ever ask for your OTP over call, email, or chat.

3. **Use strong, unique passwords** for every online account. A strong password includes uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols. Use a password manager if needed.

4. **Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)** on all important accounts including email, social media, and banking apps. Even if someone gets your password, they cannot access your account without the second factor.

5. **Keep your devices and apps updated** regularly. Software updates often include critical security patches that protect against known vulnerabilities.

6. **Avoid using public Wi-Fi** (in malls, cafes, or trains) to access banking apps or enter passwords. Attackers can intercept data on unsecured networks. Use your mobile data instead.

---

## Financial and UPI Safety (Specific Tips)

7. **Never scan a QR code to receive money** — QR codes are for sending money, not receiving it. If someone asks you to scan a code to receive a payment, it is a scam.

8. **Set a daily UPI transaction limit** on your app and regularly monitor your transaction history for any unauthorised activity. Report suspicious transactions to your bank and the UPI platform immediately.

---

## Reporting Cybercrime in India

- **National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal:** [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in)
- **Cyber Crime Helpline:** **1930** (available 24x7)

If you are a victim of online fraud, financial scam, phishing, or any cybercrime, report it immediately on cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930. The faster you report, the higher the chance of fund recovery.

---

*Remember: Staying safe online is not about fear — it is about awareness. Share these tips with your family and friends.*

# Task 5 – Cybercrime Case Study: Phishing

## Case Study: "The Fake Scholarship Email"

### Type of Cybercrime
Phishing

### What is Phishing?
Phishing is a type of cybercrime where an attacker disguises themselves as a trusted entity — such as a bank, university, or government body — to trick individuals into revealing sensitive personal information like passwords, OTPs, or banking credentials.

### How It Typically Happens (Step-by-Step)

1. **Target Selection** – The attacker identifies a group of potential victims. In this case, first-year college students who may be looking for scholarships or financial aid.

2. **Crafting the Bait** – The attacker sends a mass email with a subject like: *"Congratulations! You have been selected for the National Merit Scholarship 2025 – Claim Your ₹50,000 Now."* The email is designed to look exactly like an official government communication, complete with logos and formal language.

3. **Fake Link** – The email contains a link that leads to a fake website mimicking the National Scholarship Portal. The URL looks almost correct but contains a small spelling error (e.g., nationalscholarship-gov.in instead of scholarships.gov.in).

4. **Data Harvesting** – The student, excited about the scholarship, fills in their name, Aadhaar number, bank account details, and OTP received on their phone.

5. **Account Compromise** – Within hours, the attacker uses the stolen information to access the student's bank account and transfers funds out.

6. **Consequences** – The student loses money, their personal identity data is compromised, and they experience significant emotional distress. Recovery of funds is difficult.

### Who is Typically Targeted?
Students, elderly citizens, job seekers, and individuals unfamiliar with online fraud patterns are most commonly targeted.

### Consequences
Financial loss, identity theft, emotional trauma, and long legal recovery processes. In some cases, the compromised identity is used for further crimes.

*This is a realistic fictional scenario based on commonly reported phishing patterns in India. Source reference: cybercrime.gov.in*

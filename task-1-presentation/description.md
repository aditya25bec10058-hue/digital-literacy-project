# Task 1 – Digital Literacy Awareness Infographic

## Tool Used
Canva (canva.com)

## What the Infographic Shows

The infographic is titled **"Be Digitally Smart – A Guide for Students"** and is designed as a single-page visual resource for first-year B.Tech students. It is divided into three sections:

1. **What is Digital Literacy?** – A brief definition explaining that digital literacy is the ability to find, evaluate, create, and communicate information using digital technologies. It includes a simple icon-based layout showing key digital skills.

2. **Safe Internet Practices** – This section highlights essential safety habits such as using strong passwords, avoiding suspicious links, enabling two-factor authentication, and protecting personal data online.

3. **Professional Online Presence** – This section explains how students should maintain a clean and professional presence on platforms like LinkedIn and GitHub, and why it matters for internships and career opportunities.

## Reflection

Creating this infographic was an interesting experience. I chose Canva because it is free, browser-based, and offers many pre-built templates suited for educational content. The hardest part was deciding how much information to include — I wanted the design to be informative but not cluttered. I learned that visual communication is a skill in itself; just writing facts is not enough — the layout, colour scheme, and icons all work together to make the message clear. The most surprising thing I found was how many students are unaware of the importance of professional online profiles, which made me feel that creating this resource as a Digital Ambassador is genuinely useful.

Safe Internet Practices

Protect yourself online with these essential habits every student must follow daily.

Use strong, unique passwords for every account
Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
Never click links in suspicious emails or DMs
Review privacy settings on all platforms regularly

Cybercrime Alert for Students
Never share OTPs, Aadhaar numbers, or bank details online — even with people claiming to be from your college, bank, or government. If you face cybercrime, report it at cybercrime.gov.in or call helpline 1930 (24×7).

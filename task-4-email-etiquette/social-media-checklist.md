# Social Media Do's and Don'ts for College Students

## DO's ✅

1. **DO keep your profiles professional** – Use a clear, appropriate profile photo and a bio that reflects your academic and career goals, especially on LinkedIn and GitHub.

2. **DO think before you post** – Before sharing anything online, ask yourself: "Would I be comfortable if my professor, future employer, or parent saw this?" If not, do not post it.

3. **DO use privacy settings** – Regularly review and update the privacy settings on all your social media accounts. Limit who can see your personal information and posts.

4. **DO give credit for shared content** – If you share someone else's work, article, or image, always credit the original creator. This builds trust and avoids plagiarism.

5. **DO use social media for learning** – Follow industry leaders, researchers, and professional communities relevant to your field. Platforms like LinkedIn and YouTube can be powerful learning resources.

6. **DO report cyberbullying and hate speech** – If you witness bullying, harassment, or hateful content online, use the platform's report feature. Do not engage or escalate.

7. **DO keep login credentials secure** – Use unique, strong passwords for each platform and enable two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever available.

---

## DON'Ts ❌

1. **DON'T share personal information publicly** – Never post your home address, phone number, college ID, Aadhaar number, or financial details on social media.

2. **DON'T engage in or share misinformation** – Always verify news from credible sources before sharing. Spreading false information can have serious personal and legal consequences.

3. **DON'T post when angry or upset** – Emotional posts made in frustration are often regretted. Write it out, wait, and decide whether it truly needs to be shared.

4. **DON'T click on suspicious links in DMs** – Never click on unknown links sent by strangers, even if they appear to offer prizes, jobs, or scholarships. These are common phishing tactics.

5. **DON'T ignore digital footprint** – Everything you post online creates a permanent digital footprint. Future employers and college committees can and do search your name online.

6. **DON'T use social media during exams or important deadlines** – Mindless scrolling is one of the biggest academic productivity killers for college students.

7. **DON'T accept friend/connection requests from unknown people** – Be selective about who you connect with, especially on platforms where you share personal or academic information.
